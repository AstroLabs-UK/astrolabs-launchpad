We use TanStack Start, Supabase, and Cloudflare. Not because they're trendy. Because they solve real problems.

A lot of designers pick tools based on what they already know or what's easiest for them. That's backwards. The tool should serve the business, not the other way around.

TanStack Start lets us build sites that are genuinely fast. It handles routing intelligently. It doesn't bloat your JavaScript bundle. Pages render quickly because we're not trying to do everything in the browser. This matters for businesses where every millisecond counts.

Supabase gives us a proper database without months of infrastructure work. We can build features that actually need data without spending a fortune. Client auth, user dashboards, real-time stuff. It scales with you instead of becoming a bottleneck when your business grows.

Cloudflare means your site's fast everywhere. Not just fast if you're in London. Fast in Cornwall. Fast in Leeds. Their edge network takes care of that. We also use their tools for security, email routing, and a bunch of other things that would otherwise be headaches to set up.

These choices have consequences. They mean your site doesn't need expensive hosting. They mean maintenance is straightforward. They mean we can build things that actually work instead of spending six months fighting our tools.

We could use something else. We could build you a WordPress site. We could slap a Wix template together. But we'd be doing you a disservice. We'd be choosing based on what's convenient for us, not what's best for your business.

That's the difference between a builder and someone who actually cares about outcomes.
