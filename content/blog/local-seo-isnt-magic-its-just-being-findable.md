"SEO" sounds mysterious. People throw around buzzwords, promise rankings, charge thousands. Most of it's nonsense.

Local SEO is different. It's not about gaming algorithms. It's about being where your customers actually look.

If you're a plumber in Maidstone, you need to show up when someone searches "plumber Maidstone" right now. Not next month. Not after a six-month strategy. Now. That's the job.

Here's what actually works:

Your Google Business Profile needs to be complete and accurate. Address, phone number, hours, photos. Real photos. Not stock images. People want to see your actual work.

Your website needs to mention where you are, naturally. Not stuffed into keywords like spam. Just honest content that says who you serve and where. A page about serving East Kent. A blog post about local issues. Client testimonials that mention your town.

Get mentioned in actual local places. Local directories, community sites, local news. These aren't flashy, but they work.

Reviews matter. Lots of them. Real customers, real feedback. Negative reviews don't kill you as much as no reviews at all.

Technical stuff helps too. Your site needs to be fast, mobile-friendly, properly structured. But that's the floor, not the victory lap.

The honest truth? Local SEO takes time because trust takes time. You can't fake being a legitimate local business. You have to actually be one. But once you are, and your online presence reflects that, customers find you.

We've helped businesses across the South East get visible to people searching for them right now. It's not mysterious. It's just doing it right.
