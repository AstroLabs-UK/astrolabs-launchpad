Most web designers ask about colours and fonts. We ask different questions.

Before we design anything, we need to know what winning looks like for you. Are you trying to take bookings? Sell products? Build trust with potential clients? These aren't the same problem. Your site needs to solve your problem, not be a generic showcase.

We ask about your customers. Who are they? What do they actually care about? What problem are they trying to solve when they find your site? Most businesses haven't thought this through properly. They guess. That's how you end up with a site that looks nice but doesn't work.

We ask about your constraints. Budget matters. Timeline matters. What's your actual appetite for this? Are you going to maintain it yourself or do you need ongoing support? Being honest here saves everyone heartache later.

We ask what you're worried about. What keeps you up at night about your online presence? Sometimes it's visibility. Sometimes it's credibility. Sometimes it's "my competitors have nice sites and I look outdated." These things are worth naming.

We ask about your existing data. Do you have any idea who's visiting your current site? What they're doing? Where they're coming from? Most people don't. But this stuff is gold for building something that actually works.

We ask what success looks like in six months. Not "top of Google" or "lots of visitors." Real metrics. More enquiries? Better quality leads? Customers saying they found you online instead of word of mouth?

A good designer should be curious. They should ask hard questions because your answers matter more than their taste in typefaces. If someone skips this and jumps straight to sketches, they're building for themselves, not you.

We build for you. That means we ask.
