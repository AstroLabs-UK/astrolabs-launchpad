A slow website isn't just annoying. It's bleeding money.

When someone clicks through to your site, they've got maybe three seconds before they decide whether you're worth their time. A delay of even one second tanks your conversion rate. People bounce. They go to your competitor instead. They forget about you.

The worst part? You probably don't know it's happening.

We've audited sites for businesses across Kent and beyond. The pattern's always the same. The site loads fine on the owner's laptop at home. But on a real customer's phone, on a mediocre connection, with real traffic hitting the server? It crawls.

It's usually not one thing. It's death by a thousand cuts. Images that are way too big. JavaScript that takes forever to parse. Hosting that doesn't scale. Third-party scripts running wild. A design that looks great but loads like treacle.

The fix isn't complicated, but it requires intention. You need someone who actually cares about speed, not someone who just makes things look nice and hopes it works.

That's where we come in. We build sites that load fast by default. We use the right tools, we optimise from the start, and we measure everything. A fast site isn't a luxury—it's table stakes.

If your current site feels sluggish, you've already lost customers. Let's fix it.
